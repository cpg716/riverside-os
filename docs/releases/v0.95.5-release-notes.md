  the GitHub release after asset publication; production installation and
  physical signoff remain separate.**

- Prepared: **2026-07-27**

## Publication evidence

- The prior `2fe02440` build evidence is superseded by this rebuild and must not
  be reused as proof for the replacement assets.
- Release certification requires the remote tag, exact-commit Lint and
  Playwright gates, successful Windows and macOS packaging, non-empty assets,

- The release workflow appends the current full commit, build date, and workflow

  and still block publication on both that gate and exact-commit Playwright.

## Register workflow and presentation

- Installing or refreshing the application rejoins an existing open Register
  session instead of asking staff to open a second drawer. Idle unlock and
  workstation reattachment preserve the opening float and original close
  record.
- Daily Sales uses a dedicated receipt preview rather than the post-checkout
  Sale Complete screen. Shipping and alteration service activity appears in
  Daily Sales and Z-Report sources without being counted as merchandise sales.
- A checkout that combines new merchandise with a payment on an existing Order
  appears once in Daily Sales and Z-Reports. The merchandise sale, public Order
  number, allocated payment, remaining Order balance, full tender, and total
  collected today remain visible without creating a second transaction card.
- Payment-only receipts contain no false **Taken Today** merchandise section.
  Mixed receipts separate **Payment on Order** from the new merchandise and
  reconcile **Total charged today** to the complete tender.
- A standalone RMS account collection remains an RMS payment receipt with its
  payment line. Only a payment carrying an actual existing-Order allocation is
  presented as **Payment on Order**.
- A fully paid takeaway checkout remains a sale receipt with its merchandise,
  tax, savings, and tender even though the resulting Transaction Record is
  fulfilled. Pickup/payment-history formatting requires explicit picked-up
  line evidence or payment-on-order work.
- Customer receipts reduce fee-only service lines to the service name and
  price: **SHIPPING FEE** or **ALTERATION FEE**. Internal SKU, variation, and
  service-workflow detail is not printed for those fee-only lines.
- Product search now distinguishes active searching, no matches, unavailable
  Main Hub results, and exact scanned-SKU failures without clearing a valid
  prior selection.
- Parent products use one grouped variation surface with large sequential
  choices, preserved selections, and complete price-range loading before an
  item can be added.
- The action carousel, keypad, salesperson selector, payment workspace,
  shipping shortcut, and service-line presentation remain usable at Register
  and tablet dimensions without hidden or partially actionable controls.
- Repeated low-value Register errors are deduplicated so staff see the
  operational problem without a toast storm obscuring the sale.

## Returns, exchanges, and customer search

- Staged returns remain visible as the actual merchandise line with SKU,
  variation, negative value, and source Transaction Record.
- A reviewed return-value correction is retained on the specific returned line,
  cannot exceed the original paid value, and drives the same exchange or refund
  settlement amount while preserving original tax components.
- Customer lookup cancels stale requests and keeps exact customer selection
  stable across ambiguous scans and overlapping searches.
- Opening **Add Customer** closes the portaled Register search result panel so
  register actions cannot appear above or inside the customer intake drawer.
- Meilisearch is used only when its indexed document is at least as current as
  PostgreSQL authority; stale or unavailable search evidence falls back
  explicitly instead of being treated as current.
- The deterministic Playwright stack now disables Meilisearch and its daily
  reindex worker before startup, preventing its empty/fixture E2E database from
  replacing shared development or production indexes. GitHub Playwright lanes
  authorize Register cleanup only inside their disposable PostgreSQL service
  databases.

## Alterations intake and custody

- Register now separates **Quick Add**, which creates a tracked garment record,
  from **Fee Only**, which adds a non-taxable service charge without custody,
  due-date, pickup, or tailor-queue work.
- Full intake keeps garment choice, fitting decision, work requested, timing,
  capacity, fee, tag, and notes in one wide workspace with a pinned save action.
- Alteration services remain linked to the source merchandise or
  customer-owned garment and retain intake, status, work-item, and pickup audit
  history.
- Universal search can open the exact alteration directly into its planning and
  reassignment workspace.

## RMS Charge, Helcim, and reporting

- Register customer results show current RMS Charge eligibility evidence, while
  actual balance and approval remain part of the RMS Charge workflow.
- Standard and 90 Day RMS plans use explicit approval numbers. A weekly account
  record with no open-to-buy requires audited Manager Access before the sale can
  be recorded.
- A simple take-now sale approved by Helcim can be recorded locally after Main
  Hub confirmation is lost, prints a pending-sync receipt, and replays the exact
  checkout idempotently after reconnection. Fulfillment-dependent workflows
  remain live-recovery only.
- RMS program tenders stay distinct from ordinary card activity in Register and
  Daily Sales reporting, while non-merchandise service activity is reported
  outside merchandise sales without changing tender truth.
- Payments Operations marks stale synchronization clearly so an older completed
  import cannot be mistaken for current processor evidence.
- Payment-only and combined checkouts preserve two distinct identities: the
  payment receipt Transaction Record created today and the older public
  Order/Transaction receiving the allocation. Daily Sales and Z-Reports show
  **Payment on Order** with both references, the applied amount, tender, and
  remaining balance. **Receipt** reprints the payment event; **Detail** opens the
  target order.
- Customer receipts use **Payments toward existing orders** and public
  Order/Transaction numbers. Internal allocation keys such as composite import
  identifiers are never printed, and a split allocation cannot repeat the same
  physical tender, cash-tendered amount, or change.
- Payment assignment labels prefer the public fulfillment Order number, then a
  customer-facing imported Order number such as `O-117888`, and use the ROS
  Transaction number only when no Order number exists. Internal Counterpoint
  composite references remain hidden.
- Sales by Hour now follows the same merchandise boundary as Daily Sales:
  alteration and shipping services remain visible and reconcile through tender
  and Z-close evidence, but do not increase merchandise sales, transaction
  count, active sales hours, or average sale.
