## What staff should remember

1. When one person pays but **multiple** wedding members owe balances, the register sends **disbursements**: each entry names a **wedding member** and an **amount**.
2. The system creates the **primary payment** for the payer and **allocations** for beneficiaries; **Transaction Record balances** update through normal **recalc** — staff should **not** manually edit balances to mimic this.
3. Disputes (“who paid for what”) are resolved with **receipts** and **Transaction Records**, not ad-hoc discounts, unless manager approves.
4. Use **Wedding Deposit → Deposit Only** to collect now and build later, or **Collect & Build Orders** to prepare every funded member in one Builder. Equal deposits can be entered once and applied only to selected members.
5. Reopen the original payer's **Wedding Deposit → Review & Reprint** to resume funded members and print payer/member receipts. For a member whose Transaction is already posted, **Build More Items** starts a new Wedding Order without changing the earlier deposit, Transaction, or receipt. One **Create All Member Transactions** action posts the prepared separate member Transactions after funding succeeds.
6. Selecting a funded member directly in Register asks **Part of the Wedding Order?** The prompt may identify the original payer, but staff must explicitly apply the held source in **Pay**. The money returns to that original payer—not the beneficiary member—if a later original-card refund is approved.
7. Prompts and Builder drafts are nonfinancial. A decline or abandoned draft posts no Transaction, allocation, deposit, tax, booked activity, or receipt.
